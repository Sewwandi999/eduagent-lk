# Grade 9 English — WH Questions

## Purpose
Learners use who, what, when, where, why and how to request information. Focus on auxiliary verbs, subject–verb order, and suitable short answers. Use information-gap pair work. Support weaker learners with question frames and challenge advanced learners to write follow-up questions.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
