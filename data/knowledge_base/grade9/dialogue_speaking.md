# Grade 9 English — Dialogue and Speaking

## Purpose
Use role plays based on school, travel, shopping and public-service situations. Provide a communicative purpose, useful expressions and a clear success criterion. Assess fluency, clarity, appropriateness and interaction rather than accent imitation.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
