# Grade 9 English — Paragraph Writing

## Purpose
Teach topic sentence, supporting details, logical order and concluding sentence. Model a short paragraph, jointly improve it, and then ask learners to write independently. Use a checklist for unity, grammar, punctuation and spelling.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
