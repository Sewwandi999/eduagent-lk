# Grade 9 English — Simple Tenses

## Purpose
Review present, past and future forms for habits, completed events and plans. Use timelines, signal words and short personal examples. Include affirmative, negative and question forms. Differentiate with sentence frames and extension writing.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
