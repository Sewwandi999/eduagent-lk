# Grade 9 English — Assessment and Differentiation

## Purpose
Plan a warm-up check, guided practice, independent task and exit ticket. For support, use visuals, word banks and sentence frames. For advanced learners, require explanation, editing or original production. Feedback should identify one strength and one next step.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
