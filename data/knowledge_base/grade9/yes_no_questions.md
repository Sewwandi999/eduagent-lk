# Grade 9 English — Yes No Questions

## Purpose
Learners form questions using be, do, have and modal auxiliaries. Contrast statements and questions, practise rising intonation, and include short answers. Check word order carefully. A useful assessment is converting five statements into questions and answering them.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
