# Grade 9 English — Reported Speech Basics

## Purpose
Learners identify direct and reported statements, change pronouns and time references, and practise reporting classroom messages. Begin with short present-tense statements before introducing common backshift patterns. Use familiar school situations. Assess with sentence transformation and one original reported statement.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
