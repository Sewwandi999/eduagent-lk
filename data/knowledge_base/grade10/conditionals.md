# Grade 10 English — Conditionals

## Purpose
Teach zero and first conditionals for facts and likely future results, then introduce second conditional for unreal or less likely situations when appropriate. Use situation cards, sentence matching and personalised examples. Check comma use and verb forms.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
