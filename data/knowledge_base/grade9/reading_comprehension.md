# Grade 9 English — Reading Comprehension

## Purpose
Develop skimming for gist, scanning for details, vocabulary in context and simple inference. Questions should progress from literal information to short justification. Encourage learners to underline evidence before answering.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
