# Grade 9 English — Parts of Speech

## Purpose
Review nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs in meaningful sentences. Learners classify words and explain how changing a word class changes meaning. Avoid isolated memorisation; use short texts and colour-free underlining or labels.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
