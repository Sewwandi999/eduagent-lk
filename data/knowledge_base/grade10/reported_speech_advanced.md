# Grade 10 English — Reported Speech Advanced

## Purpose
Extend reported speech to questions, commands and requests. Learners select reporting verbs, adjust word order, remove question marks where appropriate, and change pronouns, tense and time expressions. Include error correction and contextual dialogue reporting.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
