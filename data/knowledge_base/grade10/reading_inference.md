# Grade 10 English — Reading and Inference

## Purpose
Move beyond literal answers by asking learners to infer attitude, purpose and implied meaning using textual evidence. Teach learners to quote or paraphrase the clue that supports an answer. Use short age-appropriate texts.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
