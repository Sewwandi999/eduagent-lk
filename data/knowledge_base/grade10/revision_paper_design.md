# Grade 10 English — Revision Paper Design

## Purpose
A balanced paper samples grammar, vocabulary, reading and writing. Questions should progress from accessible to challenging, use unambiguous instructions, show marks, and include a complete marking guide. Avoid testing content not taught.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
