# Grade 10 English — Relative Clauses

## Purpose
Learners combine information using who, which, that, where and whose. Begin with defining clauses and clear antecedents. Use people, places and objects familiar to students. Include sentence combining and editing unclear references.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
