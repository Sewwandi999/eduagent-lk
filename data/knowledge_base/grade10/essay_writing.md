# Grade 10 English — Essay Writing

## Purpose
Plan an introduction, logically ordered body paragraphs and a conclusion. Use brainstorming, a simple outline and paragraph-level linking. Assessment should separate content, organisation, language accuracy and mechanics. Provide peer review prompts.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
