# Grade 9 English — Passive Voice Introduction

## Purpose
Introduce the passive when the action or result is more important than the doer. Build forms with be plus past participle. Start with present and past simple examples, then compare active and passive meanings.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
