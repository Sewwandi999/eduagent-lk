# Grade 10 English — Question Tags

## Purpose
Learners form tags by matching auxiliary verbs and changing polarity. Practise pronouns and intonation for checking information versus seeking agreement. Include common irregular cases such as I am / aren’t I only when suitable for the class.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
