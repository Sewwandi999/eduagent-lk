# Grade 9 English — Conjunctions

## Purpose
Teach coordinating and subordinating conjunctions through sentence joining. Learners select connectors according to meaning: addition, contrast, reason, result, time and condition. Classroom tasks should move from matching halves to writing original compound and complex sentences.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
