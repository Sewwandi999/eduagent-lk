# Grade 10 English — Formal Letter Writing

## Purpose
Teach purpose, sender and receiver details, salutation, organised paragraphs, polite requests and formal closing. Learners analyse a model, reorder mixed sections and write a short letter for a realistic school or community purpose.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
