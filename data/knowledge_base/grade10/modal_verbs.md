# Grade 10 English — Modal Verbs

## Purpose
Teach modals for ability, permission, advice, obligation, prohibition and possibility. Compare meaning and strength in context. Use school rules, health advice and future possibilities. Avoid teaching forms without communicative situations.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
