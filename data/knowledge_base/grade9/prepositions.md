# Grade 9 English — Prepositions

## Purpose
Learners use common prepositions of time, place and movement accurately. Use classroom objects and a simple map for speaking practice. Contrast frequently confused forms and assess through gap filling followed by two original sentences.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
