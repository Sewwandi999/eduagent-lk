# Grade 10 English — Passive Voice

## Purpose
Develop active-to-passive transformation across common tenses. Learners decide when the agent is unnecessary and when a by-phrase is useful. Use process descriptions and news-style sentences. Assessment should test form and communicative choice.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
