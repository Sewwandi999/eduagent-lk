# Grade 10 English — Debate and Speaking

## Purpose
Develop claim, reason, example, rebuttal and respectful turn-taking. Give preparation time and role cards. Evaluate relevance, organisation, language clarity and response to opposing ideas. Support mixed-ability groups with sentence starters.

## Suggested lesson evidence
A classroom-ready plan should state measurable objectives, show realistic timing, include learner participation, and provide an answer key or assessment criteria.
